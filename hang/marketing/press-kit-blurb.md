# Hang — press kit

### Elevator pitch
Hang is a live map that shows you nearby strangers doing the same thing you're doing right now — turning a shared activity into a real, in-person meetup instead of another online connection.

### About Hang
Hang is a location-based social app built around a simple idea: people aren't short on connections, they're short on reasons to meet the people actually near them. Instead of a feed to scroll, Hang shows a live map of real activities happening nearby — studying, working out, grabbing coffee, practicing yoga — and lets users set their own visibility radius. Its signature feature, Sparks, automatically detects when a nearby stranger is doing the same activity as you and surfaces it in real time, creating a natural, low-pressure reason to say hello. A built-in push-to-talk Walkie-Talkie feature lets users connect by voice instantly, without the friction of a text thread. Hang's entire design philosophy points toward one outcome: getting people off their phones and into the same room.

### Quick facts
- **Sparks:** real-time detection that surfaces nearby strangers sharing your current activity — the app's core differentiator from feed-based social apps.
- **Radius control:** users choose their visibility range (1km–25km), balancing serendipity with privacy.
- **Walkie-Talkie:** one-tap, real push-to-talk voice built directly into profiles — no typing required to say hi.
