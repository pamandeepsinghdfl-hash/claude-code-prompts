# HANG
### Stop scrolling. Start showing up.

Hang is the map that shows you who's nearby doing what you're doing — right now.
Studying, stretching, grabbing coffee, pulling an all-nighter in the lab. Pick
your activity, pick your radius, and Hang quietly tells you when a stranger
nearby is doing the exact same thing. That's a **Spark** — and it's the whole
point: a real reason to say hi to someone real, in person, today.

### Why Hang

- **Sparks, not feeds.** No scrolling, no likes to chase — just a ping when
  someone near you shares what you're doing right now. The app's one job is to
  get you off the app.
- **You control the radius.** 1km or 25km, your call. See the people around
  you, not the whole internet.
- **Walkie-Talkie, not DMs.** One tap, real push-to-talk voice. Say hi like a
  human, not a text thread.
- **Built for offline.** Every feature points the same direction: get two
  people who'd never otherwise meet into the same room.
