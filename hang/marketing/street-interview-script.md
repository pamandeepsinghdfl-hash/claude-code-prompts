# HANG — "What Are You Up To Right Now?" (street interview concept)

Format: vox-pop / "man-on-the-street" interview — the viral social format where a
host walks up to strangers with one punchy question. 60–75 sec, vertical 9:16,
for Reels / TikTok / Shorts. Built to be either (a) actually filmed with a host
and real passersby, or (b) fed shot-by-shot into an AI video generator (Seedance
prompts included at the bottom for that path).

---

## Hook (0:00–0:05)
Host, handheld phone camera, walking fast down a busy sidewalk, energetic jump cut
every ~1.5s between different streets/cities.

**Host (to camera, mid-walk):** "I'm asking strangers one question: what are you
*actually* doing right now — and would you want the world to know?"

Text overlay, bold sans-serif, white on black box: **ONE QUESTION. ZERO FILTERS.**

---

## Interviews (0:05–0:55)
Six quick-cut interviews, ~7–8 seconds each, different person/vibe/city block
every time. Same two questions each time, cut to the best 1–2 lines of the answer.

**Q1 (host, off camera):** "What are you up to right now?"
**Q2 (host, off camera):** "Would you drop a pin for that — right now, on a map,
for people nearby to see?"

| # | Person / vibe | Activity | Sample answer (authentic, not scripted-sounding) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guy on a bench, headphones around neck | Study | "Honestly? Cramming for a final I should've started last week." *(laughs)* "Yeah, drop the pin — maybe somebody else is suffering with me." |
| 2 | Woman stretching outside a studio | Yoga | "Just finished a class, mat's still in my bag." "I'd want other yoga people to find me, not randoms. So — only if it's just my people." |
| 3 | Older man on a park bench, eyes closed | Prayer / spirituality | "Five minutes of quiet before the day gets loud." *(smiles)* "Sure. Maybe someone else needs five minutes too." |
| 4 | Guy outside a building, cigarette in hand | Smoking | "Taking five. Don't tell my mom." *(laughs)* "Pin it, why not — at least it's honest." |
| 5 | Two friends with coffee cups, laughing | Coffee / chill | "Nothing. Absolutely nothing. And it's great." "Drop ten pins. We're always here." |
| 6 | Student with a telescope app open / lab coat vibe | Science | "Trying to catch a meteor shower later, not even kidding." "Yeah — that's a story I *want* people to stumble into." |

Direction notes:
- Mix confident "yes" answers with one hesitant "only for my people" answer (interview
  #2) — it sets up the Walkie-Talkie/category-filter pitch honestly instead of
  pretending everyone wants to broadcast everything.
- Keep it funny and warm, not slick. The format only works if it feels real.

---

## The turn (0:55–1:05)
Cut to a clean phone-screen recording of the actual Hang app: map, avatars hopping
into place one by one, category chips filtering live, radius slider sliding from
25km down to 1km.

**Host (voiceover, calmer, direct):** "That's Hang. You pick what you're doing.
You pick who sees it. You pick how close."

---

## Walkie-Talkie beat (1:05–1:12)
Close-up: thumb presses a glowing push-to-talk button, red ring pulses, quick radio
static *chirp*.

**Host (voiceover):** "And if someone nearby's doing the same thing — just talk."

---

## CTA (1:12–end)
Logo card: **HANG** — tagline beneath: *"Show up. On your terms."*
Text overlay: **Coming soon** / or **Link in bio** (swap once you have a store link).

---

## Shot list (for a real film crew)
1. Host walking shot — gimbal or phone stabilizer, 3 different streets, golden hour.
2. 6× interview setups — single handheld camera, natural light, lav mic on host,
   on-camera mic for interviewee. Get 20–30s per person, cut to the punch line.
3. Screen recording of the app (use the existing demo, `index.html`, with a phone
   frame overlay added in post).
4. Macro/close-up of a thumb on a phone screen for the push-to-talk beat.
5. Logo card — motion graphic, 3 seconds, clean.

Music: upbeat, slightly lo-fi/indie — avoid anything that sounds like a corporate
stock-ad track; the format lives or dies on feeling unpolished-on-purpose.

## AI-video-generation path (if filming isn't an option yet)

If you have access to an AI video generator, here are production-ready prompt
blocks, one per shot. These describe photoreal human subjects in everyday public
settings — review your generator's policy on realistic people before using these,
and prefer generating b-roll/city shots this way while using short real clips (even
phone-shot) for the actual interview answers, since AI-generated "real person"
dialogue reads as fake fast and undercuts the format's whole point.

**Shot A — hook walk:**
> Handheld phone-camera footage, young host walking briskly down a sunny city
> sidewalk, mid-sentence talking to camera, candid documentary style, natural
> motion blur, golden-hour light, 9:16 vertical, realistic skin and fabric texture,
> no text overlay.

**Shot B — city b-roll, establishing:**
> Wide drone-style shot slowly descending over a busy modern city street at dusk,
> warm streetlights turning on, pedestrians crossing, cinematic color grade, teal
> and amber tones, shallow depth transitioning to sharp focus on the crowd below.

**Shot C — app screen beat:**
> Close-up of a hand holding a smartphone, screen showing a dark-themed map app
> with glowing avatar pins appearing one by one and a radius circle animating
> outward, soft blue glow reflecting on the hand, shallow depth of field, studio
> lighting, 9:16 vertical.

**Shot D — push-to-talk close-up:**
> Extreme close-up of a thumb pressing a circular glowing button on a phone screen,
> a red pulse ring animates outward on press, soft bokeh background, macro lens
> look, 9:16 vertical.
